## 3. Mechanism test: prefix mass vs unreachability (marius, k=10)

Prefix mass = total train popularity of all catalog items sharing the item's L1 (or L1:L2) semantic-ID code prefix. Items are binned into popularity quintiles (1 = least popular); within each quintile a deterministic median split on prefix mass (rank-based, ties by item index) gives the low/high columns. If beam search prunes whole prefixes at decoding step 1, low-mass-prefix items should be unreachable even at fixed own-popularity.
